About

Why Physical AI, and why now

Physical AI is artificial intelligence that perceives, decides, and acts in the real world — from humanoid robots and autonomous systems to the factories, homes, and infrastructure they will transform. LG Group views this field as a core engine of its future growth.

The LG Inhwawon MBA program has set aside three dedicated days for participants to engage deeply with this opportunity: to understand the technology, map the competitive landscape, and develop business strategies worthy of the group's next chapter.

This site is your single home base for the hackathon — the full schedule, the casebook, a strategic content map, an interactive knowledge graph, and the latest industry news, all in one place.
